CSE 430 – Introduction to Artificial Intelligence

First in a three-course sequence in Artificial Intelligence

Course Description

Admission into the program required for enrollment. First in a three-course sequence in Artificial Intelligence. Introduction to Artificial Intelligence (AI) general theories, machine learning with emphasis on techniques, theory, and algorithms, that enable computers to learn. Topics include AI’s current and future effects on society (economic, employment, social, military, politics).

Prerequisite: CSE 223 (grade of “C” or higher) and admission into the BS Computer Science program.

Course Outcomes

By the end of this course, students will be able to:

  1. Describe fundamental approaches to artificial intelligence and how they differ from traditional techniques.
  2. Demonstrate basic proficiency with popular machine learning tools: Colab, Python and Keras.
  3. Analyze and describe the ethical, social, economic, political, and military implications of AI.

Course Policies

Assignment submission

Timely submission of the assignments is crucial not only for students’ grades but also for students’ overall learning experience. Upload all your completed assignments to Canvas on or before the due date.

All due dates are final (no exception)! Late submission has a 10% penalty per day, up to a maximum of 50%.

To avoid any disruptions to your academic progress and to ensure that your efforts are duly acknowledged, please follow these steps to post your completed assignments on Canvas:

  1. Log into Canvas: access your course dashboard.
  2. Locate the Assignment: navigate to the specific course and assignment for which you want to submit your work. Be sure to double-check the due date, which will be clearly mentioned.
  3. Upload Your Assignment: click on the assignment link, and you will find an option to upload or submit your assignment. Follow the instructions provided on the assignment page.
  4. Verify Submission: after uploading your assignment, take a moment to verify that it has been successfully submitted. You should receive a confirmation message or notification.
  5. Check for Plagiarism: ensure that your work is original and properly cited. Any instances of plagiarism will result in academic consequences.

Extenuating circumstances

However, there may be occasions when you are unable to undertake, complete or hand in a piece of coursework/assignment due to circumstances beyond your control, such as being sick or a family member needing your immediate attention. Timely communication with me is very important. You can still submit work late if you have evidence of extenuating circumstances in relation to the late submission of coursework.

Extenuating circumstances are defined as the inability to submit work by the deadline due to reasons beyond your control, of a medical, practical or personal nature, which affect you in the period immediately preceding the deadline. Genuine emergencies which could not reasonably have been expected will be accepted as extenuating.
