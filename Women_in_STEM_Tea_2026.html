The Women in STEM Tea is held each spring quarter for students, faculty, community members and alumni, with about 50–75 participants. The 2026 theme, Hidden Women in STEM, highlighted the overlooked contributions of women in science, technology, engineering and math through workshops, talks and networking.

My participation

I was invited by Carol Hsu (Engineering Faculty) shortly after joining Clark College. At the event I was assigned a table, where students came to ask how to get started and move forward in computer science and AI. I guided them on the learning paths, courses and next steps available in the CSE department.
